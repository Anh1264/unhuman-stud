AIDEN VU · 2026

I build cinematic worlds without a studio behind me. Writing, direction, image and video generation, sound, edit — the whole pipeline runs through one person. I care less about "AI content" and more about marketing-literate storytelling: work that actually moves people and moves product.

I'm a CS student who codes my own production tooling, and I'm sharpening a real visual eye alongside it — studying fashion, film language, and design in public. The goal isn't to replace crews; it's to prove one focused operator can ship campaign- and festival-grade work.
